Google Quick Search

Select any word, then press a shortcut.

Shortcuts

Click a shortcut, then press the key combination. Press Esc to cancel or Backspace to clear it. Changes save automatically.

  • Search Google Open the selected text in a new Google results tab.
  • Ask Gemini Open Gemini in a new tab with the selection filled in as your prompt.

Good to know

  • Shortcuts fire whenever text is selected — on the page or inside inputs, textareas, and editable fields.
  • Ask Gemini opens Gemini with the selection pre-filled as your prompt. You may need to press Enter or click Send.
  • Shortcuts can't run on chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store, or PDF viewers.
  • Click the toolbar icon any time to open this page.
